How many rooms can you see mould in?

Count the rooms where you can see mould. Your best guess is all we need. Every room we treat is fogged as part of the process, and we confirm the exact price once we have seen your photos and had a quick chat.

Question 2 of 4

How many other rooms open onto those rooms?

Rooms that share air with a mouldy room pick up spores even when nothing is growing in them, so treating them at the same time is what stops it coming straight back. Count the rooms that open onto or share air with the rooms you just counted. If you are not sure, pick none and we will work it out on site.

Question 3 of 4

Has the mould come from a leak or a flood?

Any water ingress counts: a roof leak, a tap or pipe leak, a leaking dishwasher or washing machine, an overflowing bathtub, or flooding of any kind. We ask because water damage often needs investigating before treatment is the right step.
