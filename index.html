Question 4 of 6

In how many rooms is the mould colonised on 3 or more surfaces?

Colonised means patches of mould close together. Surfaces are the ceiling, each wall, window frames and fixtures like the shower. Example: a bathroom with mould on the ceiling, a wall and in the shower counts as one room.

Question 5 of 6

Has the mould come from a confirmed leak?

Any water ingress counts: a roof leak, a tap or pipe leak, a leaking dishwasher or washing machine, an overflowing bathtub, or flooding of any kind.
